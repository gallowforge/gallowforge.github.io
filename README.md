# gallowforge.github.io

The source of [gallowforge.github.io](https://gallowforge.github.io): a home page and one page for each project, written by hand.

What is here is what the browser gets, unminified and readable:

- `index.html`: the home page
- `work/<project>/index.html`: a page for each project, with its pictures, its numbers and a short case study
- `assets/style.css` and `assets/next.css`: the styles, dark and light
- `assets/main.js`: the theme switch, the stack in the first view, the tabs and the menu
- `assets/img/`: every product in its own dark and light theme, photographed from the running product
- `404.html`: the page for an address that does not exist

There is no framework and no tracker, and the page sets no cookie. The only thing it keeps is the theme you chose, on your own device.

The projects it shows live in their own repositories: [Holdfast](https://github.com/gallowforge/holdfast), [Seaward](https://github.com/gallowforge/seaward), [Ballast](https://github.com/gallowforge/ballast), [Ethereal](https://github.com/gallowforge/Ethereal), [Evalbench](https://github.com/gallowforge/evalbench), [Vesper](https://github.com/gallowforge/vesper), [homelab](https://github.com/gallowforge/homelab), [agent-guardrails](https://github.com/gallowforge/agent-guardrails), [aih](https://github.com/gallowforge/aih), [bwtidy](https://github.com/gallowforge/bwtidy) and [filmforge](https://github.com/gallowforge/filmforge).
